HR Analytics Dashboard

Interactive data dashboard
Problem
An HR team needs to see workforce size, salary cost and how people are spread across departments and countries without digging through raw records.
Analysis
Organised the data for 1,000 employees across 7 departments and 3 countries, then built charts for headcount, average salary, gender mix and location, with filters for hire date, department and country.
Insight
IT is the largest department at about 280 people. The USA holds 634 of the 1,000 employees. Accounting has the highest average salary and HR the lowest. The workforce is split 515 female and 485 male.
