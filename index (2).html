E-commerce Sales Analytics

Python · SQL
Problem
An online store saw flat revenue and could not tell which products, regions, or campaigns drove it.
Analysis
Cleaned 12 months of orders, then broke down revenue by category, region, and customer cohort.
Insight
A small group of repeat buyers produced most of the profit, while the heaviest discounts attracted one-time shoppers.

Customer Churn Prediction

Python · Machine learning
Problem
A subscription business was losing customers and only noticed after they left.
Analysis
Built features from usage, billing, and support history, then compared several classification models.
Insight
Churn peaked in the first 90 days and was strongly linked to repeated support tickets and failed payments.

Sales Prediction

Python · Time series
Problem
A retailer ordered stock by gut feeling, leading to shortages in peak weeks and excess inventory after.
Analysis
Modeled weekly sales with seasonality, promotions, and holidays, and tested against held-out weeks.
Insight
Promotions and seasonality explained most demand swings, so stock levels could follow a forecast.
